How It Works & Technical Details

This simulator models a Reversing Motor Starter controlled by a PLC (Programmable Logic Controller). In industrial engineering, motors cannot be instantly reversed while spinning without causing major electrical surges and mechanical stress. The logic includes an automated 5-second dwell/stop delay to let the rotor fully decelerate before reversing direction.

Program Sequence:

  • Toggle the Switch ON to close the I:0/0 contact, energizing the system control relay (M0).
  • Phase 1 (Forward): The forward contactor coil O:0/1 is energized. The motor speeds up in the clockwise direction, and the timer T1 begins its 10-second countdown.
  • Phase 2 (Dwell/Stop): Once T1 reaches 10s, its contact trips. The forward contactor de-energizes. The motor decelerates to 0 RPM. The dwell timer T2 counts down for 5 seconds.
  • Phase 3 (Reverse): Once the dwell timer T2 finishes, it triggers the reverse contactor coil O:0/2. The motor spins counter-clockwise, and T3 counts down for 10 seconds.
  • Phase 4 (Cycle Reset): When T3 completes, it triggers a cycle reset. If the switch remains ON, the system automatically loops back to Phase 1.
  • Interlocking Safety: Hardware/software interlocks (O:0/1 and O:0/2 normally-closed contacts) are programmed in series on Rungs 1 & 2. This makes it mathematically impossible to energize both forward and reverse contactors simultaneously, which would trigger a catastrophic phase-to-phase short circuit.

PLC I/O Mapping:

I:0/0 Physical Input Main Switch Main selector switch to start or halt the automatic control cycle.

O:0/1 Physical Output FWD Contactor Closes the 3-phase contacts to spin the motor Clockwise.
O:0/2 Physical Output REV Contactor Swaps two phases and closes contacts to spin the motor Counter-Clockwise.
