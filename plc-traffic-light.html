How the Traffic Light Controller Works

This simulator recreates the Delta DOP-107BV HMI panel emulator interface running alongside Delta ISPSoft PLC ladder logic for a single-lane road junction traffic signal. Industrial signals use precise hardware internal relays (M registers) and timer coils (T registers) to coordinate Red, Yellow, and Green lights safely.

Program Sequence:

  • Pressing the START PB on the HMI triggers standard latching relay M0 (Rung 0). The seal-in contact bypasses the Start button, keeping the system running.
  • Timer T0 Block: Rung 4 drives the main timer T0. It accumulates time up to 25.0 seconds (250 ticks). At 25.0 seconds, the normally-closed contact T0 opens, resetting the timer to 0 and starting the cycle again automatically.
  • 🔴 Signal 1 (Red Phase): While the timer is between 0.0s and 10.0s, the internal coil M1 is active. This closes contact M1 on Rung 1, energizing physical output Y20 (Red Light). Intersection cars detect this Red phase and decelerate to stop at the line.
  • 🟡 Signal 2 (Yellow Phase): When the timer reaches 10.0s (up to 15.0s), the phase changes. Coil M2 is energized, closing Rung 2 and activating output Y21 (Yellow Light). Cars remain stopped.
  • 🟢 Signal 3 (Green Phase): Between 15.0s and 25.0s, internal coil M3 is active, closing Rung 3 and turning on physical output Y22 (Green Light). Cars accelerate and cross the intersection.
  • STOP Operation: Pressing the STOP PB opens contact M11 (Rung 0), de-energizing the main run relay M0. The entire process halts instantly, turning all output signals and timers OFF for safety.
